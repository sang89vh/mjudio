Frequently Asked Questions

Turning an audio file into a music visualizer or lyric video on your PC.

Updated 9 September 2026

Getting started

What does Mjudio do?

You give it a song. It gives you a video — either a music visualizer, where graphics move in time with the audio, or a lyric video, where the words appear in sync with the track. You do not need any video editing experience.

What do I need to run it?

A PC running Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, 64-bit (x64). Rendering runs on your graphics hardware. The installer sets the app up for your user account only, so it does not need administrator rights.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Mjudio for Windows is free in full — no subscription, no in-app purchase, no account, and no watermark on anything you export.

Which audio formats can I import?

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC and FLAC.

Can I add my own lyrics?

Yes. Type them in, or import a timed lyric file in .lrc or .srt format. You can adjust the timing of each line inside the app.

Exporting

What resolutions can I export?

720p, 1080p, 1440p (2K) and 4K (2160p), at 30 or 60 frames per second, written as MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. Canvases can be landscape 16:9, portrait 9:16 or square 1:1.

How long does an export take?

It depends on the length of the track, the resolution and your PC. Exports are queued and written in the background — you can line up several and carry on using the app while they run.

What happens if my PC shuts down mid-export?

Exports are written in segments and checkpointed as they go, so an interrupted export can resume from the last completed segment rather than starting over.

Does the exported video look the same as the preview?

Yes. The preview and the exported file are produced by the same rendering code, so what you see while editing is what gets written to disk.

Privacy and your files

Are my songs uploaded anywhere?

No. Mjudio works entirely offline. Your audio, images, lyrics and exported videos stay on your PC, and the app makes no network requests at all. See the Privacy Policy.

Do you collect analytics?

Not in the sense people usually mean. The app writes a small diagnostic log to analytics.jsonl in its own folder at %APPDATA%\Mjudio, and that file is never transmitted anywhere. You can switch it off in Settings. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what goes in it.

Where does Mjudio keep my projects?

In %APPDATA%\Mjudio\Projects. Uninstalling the app leaves that folder alone, so your work survives an uninstall or a reinstall.

Why does the app say my audio file is missing?

Because a file it was using was moved, renamed or deleted, or is on a drive that is not connected. Open the project and point it at the file again to relink it.
