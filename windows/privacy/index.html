Privacy Policy

Mjudio for Windows does not collect your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your PC.

Effective 9 September 2026

The short version. Mjudio has no user accounts, no servers and no tracking. Your audio files, images, lyrics and finished videos never leave your computer. We cannot see them, because there is nowhere for them to go.

1. Information we collect

None. Mjudio does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information. There is no sign-up and no account.

2. Your media stays on your device

Mjudio is an offline application. The audio you import, the images and video you add, the lyrics you write and the videos you export are all read, processed and written locally on your PC. None of it is uploaded, transmitted or backed up to us or to anyone else.

Mjudio only opens files you choose yourself, through a file picker or by dragging them into the window. It does not search your disk and it does not index your music library.

3. Usage statistics stay on your device too

Mjudio records a small local diagnostic log, written as one JSON line per event to analytics.jsonl inside the app's own folder at %APPDATA%\Mjudio.

This log is never transmitted. It is not sent to us, and it is not shared with any analytics provider. It exists so that you can inspect it if you want to. You can turn the recording off entirely in Settings, and deleting that folder removes it.

What it records is technical, not personal: that the app was opened; that a project was created and from which built-in template; that a layer of a given kind was added; that audio of a given length and file type was imported; and that an export started, finished or failed, with its resolution, frame rate, codec and elapsed time. It does not contain your audio, your images, your lyrics or any text you typed.

One case is worth stating precisely rather than glossing over: when an export fails, the log records the error message the failure produced, and a Windows error message about a file can include that file's name and path — for example if a song was moved or a disk filled up. That line still never leaves your PC, and switching the recording off in Settings stops it being written at all.

4. Network activity

Mjudio makes no network requests — not to us, and not to anyone else. There is no update checker, no telemetry endpoint, no online catalog and no advertising. The app is fully functional with no internet connection, and behaves identically whether or not you have one.

Mjudio does contain one network feature, and it is incoming, local, and off until you switch it on: the optional automation API. When you enable it in Settings, Mjudio listens on a port on 127.0.0.1 so that a script running on the same PC can drive the app. 127.0.0.1 is your own machine and nothing else — the listener is not reachable from your network or from the internet, and it sends nothing anywhere.

5. Payments

There are none. Mjudio for Windows is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscription, so the app never handles payment details of any kind.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data, because we do not collect any.

Mjudio contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs and no social media trackers.

7. Children

Mjudio is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Mjudio adds a feature that requires transmitting data — for example an online template library — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. Continued use of a new version after such an update means you accept the revised policy.
